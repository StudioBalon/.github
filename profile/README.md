**Balon** (בלון, say *BAlon*) is a small web studio in Israel. We design and build websites for local businesses: the studio down the street, the clinic, the carpenter, the café. Each site is made from the business itself, its photos, its words and what it's known for, never from a template.

We work in Hebrew first, mobile first, and we keep it simple for the owner: one fair price, nothing to pay until the site is live, and help when something needs changing.
